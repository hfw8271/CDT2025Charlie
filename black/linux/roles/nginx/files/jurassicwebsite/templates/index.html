Step into a world where dinosaurs roam the Earth again. From towering T-Rexes to gentle Brachiosauruses, Jurassic Park brings prehistoric life to your fingertips.

The Story Behind the Park

Founded by visionary billionaire John Hammond, Jurassic Park is located on Isla Nublar, an island 120 miles west of Costa Rica. Utilizing groundbreaking genetic engineering from the labs of InGen, the park has successfully cloned dozens of dinosaur species, reviving creatures long extinct for over 65 million years.

Jurassic Park is more than a theme park — it's a scientific marvel and a testament to the power of modern science and ambition. Guests can explore the park via safari vehicles, river cruises, or sky trams for a one-of-a-kind immersive journey through prehistory.
